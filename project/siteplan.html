Website Planning Document

Site Name

Nails by Notty

This name represents my mobile nail business that provides luxury nail services directly to clients at their homes, workplaces, hotels, and special events. It is personal, memorable, and reflects the professional image of the brand.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote Nails by Notty and make it easy for clients to learn about the business, browse services, view pricing, see examples of nail designs, and book appointments through WhatsApp.

Audience Scenarios

  • How do I book a mobile nail appointment?
  • What are the prices for gel tips, overlays, and gel toes?
  • Which areas do you travel to?
